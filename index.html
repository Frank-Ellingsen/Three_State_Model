Recommended Action Memorandum

TO: Board of Directors & Executive Management
FROM: Frank Ellingsen (Financial Controller / Project Specialist)
DATE: October 10, 2026
SUBJECT: Financial Turnaround & Operational Action Plan — AMOK EQUIPMENT AS

1. Executive Diagnostic & Financial Position

Following the 2025 reporting period, AMOK EQUIPMENT AS faces operational profitability pressure despite maintaining a strong balance sheet equity cushion (73.5% equity ratio). Operating revenues decreased by 20.3% to NOK 16.23M, triggering an operating loss (EBIT) of -NOK 1.08M and a net loss of -NOK 0.70M.

2. Core Vulnerabilities & Operational Bottlenecks

  • Severe Working Capital Lockup: Inventory stands at NOK 13.31M (>82% of revenue), consuming operating cash flow and creating obsolescence risk.
  • Fixed Overhead Rigidity: Personnel expenses (NOK 4.39M) and non-personnel operating expenses (NOK 12.92M) were not downscaled to match volume contraction.
  • Cash Drain: Cash and bank reserves declined from NOK 9.02M in 2023 to NOK 6.91M in 2025.

3. Recommended Action Plan (Prioritized Roadmap)

  • Phase 1 — Inventory Monetization & Cash Clearance (Months 1–3): Implement an immediate 15–20% inventory clearance campaign targeting slow-moving equipment lines to release NOK 3.0M–4.0M in liquidity.
  • Phase 2 — Direct Operating Cost Restructuring (Months 3–6): Re-negotiate supplier cost-of-goods agreements and target a 5% reduction in non-personnel operating expenses to restore positive EBIT margins.
  • Phase 3 — Credit Facility & Cash Buffer Protection (Immediate): Secure a standby credit revolver of NOK 5.0M with commercial banks while equity is strong (73.5%) to insulate against downside risk under Bear/Severe Stress scenarios.
  • Phase 4 — Strict Capital Discipline: Cap annual CapEx to essential maintenance (<2% of revenue) until operating margin returns above 5.0%.

4. Scenario Contingency Outlook

Under the active Base Case forecast, revenue recovers to NOK 28.47M by 2030 with EBITDA of NOK 1.54M and ending cash of NOK 9.08M. However, if market demand deteriorates into a Severe Stress scenario (-15% volume, -2% price), cash is depleted by 2028, requiring full drawdown of the NOK 5.0M credit facility. Immediate execution of Phase 1 and 2 is mandatory.
